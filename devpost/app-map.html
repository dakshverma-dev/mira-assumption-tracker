The working loop

Thesis & assumptions→Linked evidence→Scenario→Saved record
1. The original belief and its inputs

src/demo/aster.ts → createAster creates the fictional thesis and four stable-ID assumptions. src/domain/types.ts → getDrivers derives calculator inputs without changing the stored records.

The editors in src/components/Editors.tsx save a complete valid baseline; they do not mutate a historical scenario.

2. The evidence remains attached

EvidenceEditor captures the observation separately from the analyst's classification and explanation. src/App.tsx filters by assumption ID, so renaming a company cannot break a link.

src/domain/schema.ts → validateWorkspace rejects dangling references, duplicate IDs, impossible dates and non-web source URLs.

3. A transparent scenario

src/domain/calculate.ts → calculateScenario validates the base and independent overrides, then calculates revenue × (1 + growth)years × margin. A zero base yields no percentage instead of a division error.

src/components/Projection.tsx plots yearly operating profit with an accessible text description. tests/domain.test.ts checks the exact £32.2102m / £22.7498348192m fixture and invalid inputs.

4. A record you can revisit

saveScenario in src/App.tsx copies the original base, overrides and result into a snapshot. Current baseline edits cannot rewrite it.

src/lib/storage.ts writes only validated data. src/lib/importExport.ts validates a whole file before the UI offers replacement. Read failures preserve existing bytes and pause autosave.

A practice to reuse

Trace one surprising output back through its input, transformation and persistence boundary. Keep a known numerical fixture beside the pure function. Test the boundary case—here, zero base profit—before polishing the result.
