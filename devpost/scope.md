# Mira Assumption Tracker

A small research workspace connecting an investment thesis, the evidence behind its assumptions and a transparent operating-profit scenario.

## Planning provenance

Fresh project created for this event. Source requirements: the user-supplied four-hackathon pack's Mira brief. The user selected Mira and explicitly delegated further product and implementation decisions, asking to proceed without questions. This document is an agent-authored plan under that delegation. It is not a record of a completed learner interview or individual review. Planning status remains draft until actually reviewed; execution proceeds under the user's explicit delegation.

## The Unique Kernel

Follow a contrary observation from an assumption to a changed scenario, while keeping the original case intact. Make the causal chain inspectable rather than presenting an unexplained investment score.

## Who It's For

An investor recording why a company's growth and margin assumptions might hold, and revisiting those beliefs when evidence changes. This is a fictional research demonstration, not a market-data service or investment recommendation.

## The Core Loop

Load fictional Aster Products → inspect an assumption → attach and classify evidence → change growth or margin in a separate scenario → compare operating profit → save, refresh and export a portable record.

## Inspiration & Identity

Agent-selected direction: a calm blue-and-white analyst workspace, precise typography, compact assumption selectors and a two-line profit projection. The chart explains the financial effect; there are no decorative scores or invented confidence ratings.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The user wants a competitive hackathon submission. Personal motivation and learning reflection are not invented.

## What "Working" Looks Like

Aster's base operating profit is £32.21m in year five. Changing growth from 10% to 6% and margin from 20% to 17% produces £22.75m, down about 29.37%. The original values remain intact; evidence, saved scenarios and review reminders survive refresh and JSON round trips.

## The POC Boundary

One page, one thesis per local workspace, four drivers, manually classified evidence, separate scenario overrides, saved snapshots, dated review reminders, validated local persistence and JSON transfer. Fresh React/TypeScript application with no backend.

## Later

Multiple companies, collaboration, market feeds and Excel integration require separate research and implementation.

## Explicitly Cut

No runtime model, automatic evidence verdicts, valuation or investment-return claims, authentication, database or live monitoring. They do not help prove this small loop.
